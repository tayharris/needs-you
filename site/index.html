How it works

  1. Agents post. A Claude session, a cron job or a CI step says “I need you for this” with one command.
  2. The pill shows it. It floats over everything on your Mac and never takes focus from what you're typing.
  3. It goes away when handled. The sender clears it, or you click Done. Nothing piles up.

Install

Download for Mac

The latest release has NeedsYou-<version>.dmg (open it, drag the app to Applications) and the same app as a .zip. macOS 14 or later.

The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch. Right-click the app → Open → Open. On macOS 15 and later, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Then add your other machines. In the app, Invite a machine gives you a link; on the machine, run:

curl -fsSL <join_url>/install.sh | bash -s -- --yes

Open source

The app, the hub and the sender CLI are on GitHub. Apache-2.0. No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry.
